PaintAlong

Support

PaintAlong turns a photograph into a painting plan — watercolour, acrylic or oil: which paints to mix, how far to thin them, how to move the brush, and what the paper should look like after every move.

Getting started

  • Open the app and tap Choose a photograph, or Use the one above to try the sample.
  • Reading a photograph takes a few seconds. It is doing real work — finding the value structure, clustering the colours, and matching each one to actual paints.
  • Use the slider, the arrows, or the filmstrip to step through the moves.

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes, entirely. There is no networking code in the app at all. Aeroplane, hillside, anywhere.

Why do the three media look so different?

Because they are. Watercolour is transparent: a wash filters the paper rather than covering it, so it can only ever darken — which is why it is painted light to dark and why a white you lose never comes back. Acrylic and oil are opaque: they cover what is under them, start on a canvas toned to a mid value, and have white paint, so nothing has to be reserved. Oil keeps the record of every stroke in the surface; acrylic sets flat in minutes. Each is planned and painted on its own terms.

The mixes name paints I do not own.

Each one is listed with its Colour Index name — PB29, PBr7 and so on — which is printed on every tube regardless of brand. Match that and the colour is the same whoever made it.

It says my photograph will be difficult.

That is the feature working. Flat values, no highlight worth protecting, or detail spread evenly with nowhere for the eye to rest will all waste an afternoon, and it is better said before you start than after.

The painting does not look exactly like my photograph.

It is not meant to. A plan simplifies a photograph into a handful of mixes a person can actually lay down — that simplification is the whole point of it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

PaintAlong collects no data. None at all. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no network connection.

Your photographs

When you choose a photograph, iOS hands the app that single image. The app never requests access to your photo library, and it cannot see any other photograph. The image stays in memory on your device, is used to work out the painting plan, and is gone when you close the app or choose another one. It is never uploaded, never stored, and never shared.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app contains no networking code and makes no connections of any kind. All of the analysis and all of the painting is arithmetic performed on your phone.

Third parties

There are none. The app uses no advertising, no analytics and no third-party software development kits.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and, since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be updated here, with the date above. Any version of the app that started collecting data would say so plainly before it did.
